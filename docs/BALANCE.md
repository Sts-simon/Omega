## Reading the table

- A larger dry mass raises launch mass and the fictional cost.
- Propellant is wet mass but is consumed during flight; tanks and motors are the only parts that carry it.
- Positive power draws from the mission bus; a negative figure supplies it.
- Complexity raises the delivery pressure and lowers aerodynamic reliability; the reliability column is a relative contribution, not a percentage chance by itself.
